About

I'm a generative AI researcher. My M.Sc. work tackled mirror inpainting: generating reflections in a mirror that stay geometrically consistent with the visible scene, instead of letting the model hallucinate them. I'm now interested in exploring robotics and reinforcement learning, where I'd like to apply that same way of thinking to planning and decision-making in physical, embodied systems.

During my time in the army, I worked across a few different roles: planning operations, executing them directly, and building the technical systems behind them. I later moved into academic research for my M.Sc. Across all of them, I kept circling back to the same core problem: visual data collection, planning, and analysis. That range of experience shapes how I approach research now, breaking down ambitious goals into rigorous, verifiable steps.
