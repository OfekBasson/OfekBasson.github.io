About

I'm a generative AI researcher. My M.Sc. work tackled 3D-consistent text-to-image synthesis — treating multi-view consistency as a constraint-satisfaction problem — and I'm now moving my research focus toward robotics and reinforcement learning, where I want to apply that same constraint-driven approach to planning and decision-making in physical, embodied systems.

I bring a somewhat unusual point of view to this work: recurring exposure to visual collection, planning, and analysis from two very different directions — operationally, during my time in the army, and academically, during my M.Sc. research. Before returning to research, I spent several years leading technical and operational work in the defense technology sector: building production software for intelligence systems, planning and coordinating complex multi-team operations, and operating high-performance, safety-critical systems that demanded precise, real-time decision-making under tight constraints. That background shapes how I approach research — breaking down ambitious goals into rigorous, verifiable steps.

Research Interests
